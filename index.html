About the practice

Tomás McKenna set up McKenna Consulting Engineers in Miltown Malbay in November 2010. He is a chartered civil and structural engineer with more than 20 years as a site engineer, design engineer and project engineer.

The team works from our office on The Square, with engineers, planning and surveying consultants and office staff under one roof.
