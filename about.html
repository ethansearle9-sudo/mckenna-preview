About us

A chartered civil and structural engineering practice, working from Miltown Malbay since 2010.

Tomás McKenna set up McKenna Consulting Engineers in November 2010. We work from The Square in Miltown Malbay, Co. Clare, on civil and structural engineering projects across Ireland.

Tomás is a chartered civil and structural engineer with more than 20 years as a site engineer, design engineer and project engineer, seeing projects through planning, design and construction.

As a firm we have worked on large structural engineering projects, on sewerage, drainage and water distribution networks, and on general site infrastructure. Our building work covers new schools and school extensions, commercial buildings and homes.
